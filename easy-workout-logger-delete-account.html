Delete Your Account and Data

This page explains how to delete your account for the Easy Workout Logger app (hereby referred to as "Application"), created by Offleg (hereby referred to as "Service Provider"), and what happens to your data when you do.


How to delete your account

You can request deletion of your account directly from within the Application:

  1. Open the Easy Workout Logger app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Under Danger zone, tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

If you are unable to access the app, you can also request deletion by emailing contact@offleg.com from the email address or with the phone number associated with your account.


What happens when you delete your account

When you delete your account:

  • You are immediately signed out of the Application on all devices.
  • Your sign-in credentials (phone number, email address, and any linked Google account) are removed from our authentication provider and can no longer be used to sign in to your account.
  • Your profile is immediately marked inactive in our database.
  • App data stored locally on your device is cleared.


Data retained after account deletion

Your workout and exercise history is retained in deactivated form after your account is deleted, so that it is not accessible to you or shown in the Application, but is not immediately and automatically erased from our systems. If you would like this retained history permanently and completely erased, please email contact@offleg.com and the Service Provider will erase it within a reasonable time.


Contact Us

If you have any questions about account or data deletion, please contact the Service Provider via email at contact@offleg.com.
