Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Foodprints app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by OffLeg (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".


Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include information such as

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
  • The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages
  • The time spent on the Application
  • The operating system you use on your mobile device


The Application does not gather precise information about the location of your mobile device.


What the Application stores. To keep your food memories safe and available on every device you sign in on, the Application stores what you enter: your meals, dishes, restaurant names and locations you type, ratings, notes, tags, prices, the dish photos you add, and the name, city and profile picture you choose to add to your profile. If you answer one of the Application's short in-app questions (for example "What would make you use Foodprints more often?"), your answer and anything you type with it are stored too. This information is kept on your device and on the Service Provider's servers (hosted by Supabase), linked to your account. Dish photos and profile pictures are stored privately and are not public.


Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI). Two optional features use Google's Gemini AI, through the Service Provider's own servers:

  • Bill scanning: when you scan a restaurant bill, the photo of the bill is sent to Google Gemini to read the dish names, prices and restaurant. The Service Provider does not keep the bill photo after it has been read.
  • Voice quick add: your phone's built-in speech recognition (provided by Apple or Google) turns what you say into text. Before that text is sent to Google Gemini to understand which dishes you mentioned, the Application removes phone numbers, email addresses, card numbers and Aadhaar numbers from it. Your voice recording itself is not sent to the Service Provider.

Google processes this information under its own terms and privacy policy, available at https://policies.google.com/privacy. These features are used only when you choose them, and the information sent is not used by the Service Provider for advertising.


Permissions. The Application asks for access to your camera (to scan bills and photograph dishes), your photos (to add existing photos and choose a profile picture), your microphone and speech recognition (for voice quick add), and notifications (for optional evening reminders and an occasional one-question survey). Each is asked for only when you first use that feature, and you can refuse or withdraw it in your device settings; the rest of the Application keeps working. On iPhone, if you answer "Not now" to the Application's reminder question, reminders may still be delivered quietly to your notification list, without sound or banner; you can turn them off in the Application's Settings or in your device settings. Reminders are scheduled on your device and are not sent from the Service Provider's servers.


The Service Provider may use the information you provided to contact you from time to time to provide you with important information, required notices and marketing promotions.


For a better experience, while using the Application, the Service Provider may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information, including but not limited to your phone number and email address, which are used for account authentication and login purposes. The information that the Service Provider request will be retained by them and used as described in this privacy policy.


Third Party Access

The Service Provider uses these service providers to run the Application, and shares with them only what each needs:

  • Supabase stores your account and the information you enter (see "What the Application stores").
  • Google Gemini reads scanned bills and understands voice quick add (see "Use of Artificial Intelligence").
  • Sentry receives crash and error reports, which include technical details about your device and the Application, and an internal account identifier (not your name, email or phone number), so problems can be found and fixed.

The Service Provider may also share your information with third parties in the other ways described in this privacy statement. The Service Provider does not sell your information.


The Application includes the Meta (Facebook) SDK, which the Service Provider uses to measure the results of advertising for the Application. Through it, Meta is informed when the Application is installed, when it is opened, and once, the first time a meal is saved. That milestone event carries no details of what was saved: no dish names, restaurant names, photos, notes or ratings are sent to Meta at any point. For this measurement, Meta may process device and advertising identifiers. Meta's handling of that information is governed by its own privacy policy, available at https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/. On iOS, the Application does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and does not do so.

Staff Access

A small number of Service Provider staff can view an account's details (such as email address or phone number, name, city, sign-in method and join date), its meals, dishes, photos and profile picture, and its crash reports. They do so only to provide support, to keep the Application safe, and to remove photos that break the rules (for example offensive or illegal images). Staff can delete such a photo; they cannot change your meals or other information. Every time staff view a person's information or delete a photo, it is recorded in an audit log that cannot be edited. Staff access requires a two-step sign-in (an account and a one-time code).
